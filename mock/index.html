Player installer: look review

Mock of the one-window app that adds Sora to the player's own US disc image. These screens are the real app's HTML and CSS (app/ui/), with fake progress. Everything here is our own: sky gradient, stars, clouds, the star emblem, system fonts. No game art, no logos, no official-looking lettering.

Open the clickable demo ↗

In the demo, click the drop area or drop any file onto the window. A file name with “EU” in it shows the wrong-region message. The small “mock:” buttons at the bottom right jump straight to any screen.
